/ About
Our Story

Why engineering first is a business model, not a slogan.

Most telecom hardware companies start with a product catalogue and work backwards to a story. Saga started differently: with a thesis about what clients in this market actually need, and why most vendors aren't able to deliver it.

The thesis is straightforward. Enterprise and carrier-edge switch buyers don't just need a box that passes traffic. They need a platform they can build on — firmware they can customize, a supply chain they can rely on when allocation gets tight, and a technical relationship that doesn't reset every time they launch a new product generation.

"We're not a marketing-driven company. We put resources into engineering and inventory because that's where we actually create value for clients."

That means we focus on two chipset ecosystems — Marvell Prestera for access-layer switching, CENTEC for aggregation, core, and data center — and we go deep on both. We optimize feature sets continuously. We hold strategic buffer stock on key chipsets. We do firmware and management UI customization at a depth most suppliers won't touch.

The trade-off is transparency: we don't compete on every deal, and we're not the right partner for every buyer. We work best with clients who are optimizing for total cost of deployment, architectural consistency, and long-term supply predictability — not whoever quotes the lowest unit price this quarter.

Saga is based in Shenzhen. We work with OEM and ODM clients, system integrators, ISPs, and distributors across global markets. Our relationships are built to last, and we take the long view on every account we pursue.

Capabilities

Chipset expertise, supply-chain depth, and customization at scale.

Chipset Platforms

Two ecosystems. One commitment.

We go deep on Marvell and CENTEC — not broad across every available silicon.

Our product and engineering focus is concentrated on two chipset families. That concentration is intentional: it's what allows us to optimize feature sets continuously, hold meaningful inventory positions, and provide technical support that goes beyond datasheet reading.

Primary Platform — Access Layer

Marvell Prestera

AC5 Lite · AC5 · AC5+ — L2+ Managed Switching
  • Commercial & industrial grade — both deployment contexts covered
  • 8 / 16 / 24 / 48-port configurations · PoE / PoE+ / PoE++ (IEEE 802.3af/at/bt, up to 90W/port)
  • Dual PSU support · hot-swappable power modules — zero downtime on power failure
  • ERPS G.8032 ring protection — sub-20ms convergence · STP / RSTP / MSTP
  • QinQ double-tagging (port / VLAN / flow-based) · Private VLAN · Voice VLAN
  • IGMP v1/v2/v3 snooping · PIM-SM multicast · MLD snooping · Fast Leave
  • 802.1X + RADIUS + TACACS+ · DAI · IP Source Guard · DDoS protection
  • SFTP / SCP / SSH2 (public key) · HTTPS · SNMP v2c/v3 · sFlow · MQTT telemetry
  • Dual firmware partition · optical DDM monitoring · one-click fault collection
  • Firmware actively developed — V7.5.x roadmap, continuous feature optimization
Primary Platform — Aggregation / Core / Data Center

CENTEC

L3 Aggregation · Core · Data Center — 10G to 800G
  • Full L3 hardware-forwarded routing — OSPF, BGP, static routing
  • VXLAN overlay networking — data center fabric support
  • MPLS — label switching and traffic engineering
  • MLAG — two-active stacking, cross-chassis link aggregation
  • ACL · QoS · LACP hardware offload at line rate
  • Port speeds: 10G SFP+ · 25G SFP28 · 40G QSFP+ · 100G QSFP28 · 400G QSFP112 · 800G OSFP
  • Redundant power (CRPS) · hot-swap smart fan modules
  • Suitable for enterprise aggregation, campus core, MAN, and data center ToR/spine
  • Active platform development — strong R&D continuity

Realtek-based products also available as secondary line — primarily for price-sensitive buyers or supplementary SKU coverage. Suitable for standard L2 requirements where advanced feature depth is not the primary requirement.

Supply Chain

We manage uncertainty so you don't have to.

Strategic inventory isn't a cost center for us — it's a client value proposition. When chipset allocation tightens, our clients ship on schedule while others wait months. That reliability has a real dollar value that unit price comparisons don't capture.

Strategic Inventory

Buffer stock held against key Marvell and CENTEC chipsets. Not speculative — planned against client demand forecasts and lifecycle position of each platform.

Lifecycle Planning

We track chipset lifecycle status actively. Clients are informed well ahead of EOL transitions so product roadmaps can be adjusted without emergency re-spins.

Fast Lead Time

Inventory on hand means orders move quickly. For standard configurations, typical lead times are materially shorter than going direct to factory without buffer stock in place.

Shortage Risk Mitigation

Global chip shortages hit unprepared supply chains hard. Our inventory position is the hedge against that — built deliberately over time, not scrambled for at spot-market prices.
